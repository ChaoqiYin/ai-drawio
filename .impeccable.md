## Design Context

### Users
This product is for local desktop users who use AI-assisted draw.io sessions to inspect, edit, and iterate on diagrams in place. They need to move quickly between conversation context, canvas edits, and visual validation without losing orientation.

### Brand Personality
Clear, efficient, professional. The interface should feel dependable and calm, not flashy or playful. Visual polish should support comprehension first.

### Aesthetic Direction
Prefer light surfaces with cool blue-gray and blue-green accents. Use restrained contrast, stable spacing, and readable hierarchy. Avoid loud gradients, heavy decoration, and presentation-first styling that competes with the diagram content.

### Design Principles
1. Overview first, detail second: multi-page diagrams should read as a clear narrative across pages.
2. Efficiency over spectacle: use visual hierarchy to speed comprehension, not to show off.
3. Calm color system: use a cohesive light palette with purposeful accent colors for grouping and emphasis.
4. Diagram readability is non-negotiable: spacing, routing, and grouping must stay clear after any visual refinement.
5. Consistency across pages matters more than local flourish within one page.
